RENTAL AGREEMENT LEGAL REFERENCE
==================================

Common Provisions and Tenant Rights under Indian Law
(Transfer of Property Act, 1882 & State Rent Control Acts)

1. SECURITY DEPOSIT
   - Security deposit should typically not exceed 2-3 months' rent (varies by state).
   - Must be refunded within 1-3 months after vacancy, minus legitimate deductions.
   - Deductions must be documented with receipts (damage repair, unpaid rent).

2. RENT ESCALATION
   - Annual rent increase should be capped at 5-10% as per local rent control laws.
   - Any increase must follow proper notice period (typically 1-3 months).
   - Escalation clause must be explicitly agreed upon in writing.

3. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
   - Structural repairs are the landlord's responsibility.
   - Minor day-to-day maintenance is typically the tenant's responsibility.
   - Landlord must provide habitable premises (functional plumbing, electricity).

4. LOCK-IN PERIOD
   - Lock-in periods of 6-12 months are standard.
   - Periods exceeding 12 months may be considered unreasonable.
   - Early exit penalties should be proportionate (typically 1-2 months' rent).

5. NOTICE PERIOD
   - Standard notice period is 1-3 months for termination.
   - Both parties should have equal notice period rights.
   - Notice must be given in writing.

6. EVICTION PROTECTIONS
   - Landlord cannot evict without proper legal notice.
   - Grounds for eviction must be specified (non-payment, misuse, personal need).
   - Tenant has right to contest eviction in court.

7. SUBLETTING
   - Subletting rights should be clearly stated.
   - If prohibited, landlord's consent clause for guests should be reasonable.

8. UTILITIES AND COMMON AREAS
   - Meter reading should be documented at move-in and move-out.
   - Common area maintenance charges should be specified upfront.
   - Utility disputes should have a resolution mechanism.
