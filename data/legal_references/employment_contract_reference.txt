EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT LEGAL REFERENCE
=======================================

Common Provisions and Employee Rights under Indian Law
(Industrial Disputes Act, 1947; Shops & Establishments Acts; Labour Codes, 2020)

1. PROBATION PERIOD
   - Standard probation: 3-6 months, extendable to 12 months maximum.
   - Employee should receive written confirmation of completion or extension.
   - Termination during probation requires minimum 1 month notice or pay in lieu.
   - Probation terms must be clearly stated in appointment letter.

2. NOTICE PERIOD
   - Standard notice period: 1-3 months.
   - Notice period should be mutual (same duration for employer and employee).
   - Garden leave provisions should be compensated at full pay.
   - Notice buy-out clauses must be explicitly agreed upon.

3. NON-COMPETE CLAUSES
   - Post-employment non-compete clauses are generally unenforceable in India (Section 27, Indian Contract Act).
   - Non-solicitation clauses for a reasonable duration (6-12 months) may be enforceable.
   - Restrictive covenants during employment are valid if reasonable.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND IP
   - Confidentiality obligations should survive termination for a defined period.
   - IP assignment clauses should clearly distinguish between work-related and personal creations.
   - Trade secret protections must be specific (not overly broad).

5. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
   - Salary structure (basic, HRA, allowances) must be clearly broken down.
   - Bonus terms should specify conditions and timelines.
   - Gratuity is mandatory after 5 years of continuous service.
   - PF and ESI contributions must comply with statutory requirements.

6. TERMINATION
   - Termination for cause must specify valid grounds.
   - Retrenchment requires compliance with Industrial Disputes Act procedures.
   - Severance pay requirements vary by tenure and applicable law.
   - Full and final settlement should be completed within 30-45 days.

7. LEAVE POLICY
   - Minimum earned leave: 15-21 days per year (varies by state).
   - Sick leave and casual leave must comply with local Shops & Establishments Act.
   - Maternity leave: 26 weeks (Maternity Benefit Act, 2017).
   - Leave encashment policy must be documented.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
   - Internal grievance mechanism should be specified.
   - Arbitration or mediation clauses are preferable to direct litigation.
   - Jurisdiction should be clearly stated (typically where workplace is located).
